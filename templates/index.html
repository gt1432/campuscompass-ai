ESTABLISHED 2013 • BENGALURU

Presidency University

Presidency University, Bengaluru was established under Karnataka Act No. 41 of 2013 and is located on a sprawling green campus at Itgalpur, Rajanakunte, Yelahanka, Bengaluru.

The university is dedicated to excellence in teaching and research across diverse disciplines with world-class infrastructure, industry-collaborated programs, and comprehensive student welfare support.
